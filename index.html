How it works

  1. Send your details. Your services, service area and common questions — about a 5-minute text.
  2. Forward unanswered calls. Turn on "forward when unanswered/busy" on your phone to the number we give you. Jeremy walks you through it (~2 minutes). You keep your number.
  3. The AI answers. In your business name, it says it's an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded.
  4. You get an email summary within about a minute of every call — then you call the customer back.

What it collects

  • Caller's name
  • Callback number (read back to confirm)
  • Address or city
  • Service needed
  • Urgency
  • Best time to call back

What it never does: quote prices or book jobs without you.

Questions

Is it legal? Do callers know they're being recorded?

The AI tells every caller up front that it's an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded. Oregon is a one-party-consent state for phone calls (ORS 165.540), and the up-front notice also covers callers from all-party-consent states like California and Washington. This isn't legal advice.

What if the caller wants to talk to a real person?

The AI says honestly that it's an AI assistant taking messages for you, takes their details, and lets them know you'll call back. It never pretends to be human.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number. You just turn on "forward when unanswered/busy" on your phone, and only the calls you miss go to the AI.

Will it quote prices or book jobs?

No. It never quotes prices or books without you — it just takes the details so you can call back.

What if it's not for me?

Try it free for 7 days with no card. You only pay the setup fee if you keep it, and you can cancel anytime.
